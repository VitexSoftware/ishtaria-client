Quaternius characters

Created by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), published on Poly Pizza
(https://poly.pizza/u/Quaternius): animated, rigged player characters with their
own textures and animation clips. Each model was downloaded from Poly Pizza and
saved as a standalone GLB under a lower-case identifier. adventurer.glb is the
original file; the others were converted to GLB through Blender's glTF exporter.
